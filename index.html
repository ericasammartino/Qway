Album out September 25, 2026

About: What started as a few ideas for songs that we collectively loved and wanted to cover, turned into a full endeavor to rework each song in a way that felt unique to us and added something to the song's story. Red Clay and Red are both incredible pieces by Freddie Hubbard and King Crimson, respectively, and we hope listeners enjoy our takes on these favorites of ours. Both red in sound and in name; therefore, we call this EP "Rouge."
