Sunday Columbus Avenue

Apple season at the 79th Street Greenmarket.

Farmers set up from the museum steps toward the park. Macouns, asters, and a cider press near 79th — bring a tote and get there before the good cheese is gone.

  • When Sunday, 8am–4pm
  • Where Columbus, 77th to 81st
  • Cost Free to wander
See who’s at the market

01 Hear

The late set at Smoke.

A small room on Broadway where the Monday band still plays like the block is listening. Come for the horn section. Stay because nobody rushes the table.

Smoke Jazz & Supper Club
Broadway & 106th · Doors 6pm

02 Eat

Linden just opened on Amsterdam.

A new corner room: wood-fired vegetables, a rye loaf you can smell from the crosswalk, and a short list of wines. First-week tables disappear after seven.

Linden
Amsterdam & 84th · Dinner from 5

03 See

Puppets in the park.

The Swedish Cottage Marionette Theatre is running a short autumn show. Forty minutes, a wooden stage, and the front row whispering the plot out loud.

Swedish Cottage
Central Park · Sat & Sun, 11am
